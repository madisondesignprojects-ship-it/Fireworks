Read official fireworks safety guidance ↗
01

Check the rules first

Fireworks rules vary by location. Confirm current local and state requirements before buying, possessing, or using fireworks.

02

Choose a clear, open area

Keep spectators at a safe distance and stay clear of buildings, vehicles, dry vegetation, and overhead hazards.

03

Follow the label every time

Use products only as directed. Never relight a dud, never hold fireworks that are not designed to be held, and keep water nearby.

04

Keep kids and pets protected

Adults should handle fireworks. Keep children and pets away from the launch area and provide a comfortable place to watch from a distance.
