A Safe Celebration Is A Better Celebration

Safety First.
Every Time.

Fireworks laws vary across Wisconsin. Before buying or using any fireworks, check your local rules, read all product instructions, and set up a safe area for spectators.

Official Fireworks Safety Tips ↗
1
Check local rules

Confirm state and municipal requirements for possession and use. Items available for sale are not necessarily legal to use everywhere.

2
Give fireworks plenty of room

Use only in an open area as allowed by the product instructions, with spectators at a safe distance.

3
Never relight a dud

Follow the label, keep water nearby, and never try to relight or handle a firework that failed to function as intended.

4
Keep children away from fireworks

Adults should handle fireworks. Keep children and pets away from the firing area and let everyone enjoy the show from a safe distance.
